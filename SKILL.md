## Panel-specific overlay

The transformed design is one extremely simplified geometric pictogram. Keep only three to five identity-defining structural clues and rebuild them as a few coarse, clear, modular masses with soft corners. Recognition comes from closure, notches, negative cuts and gaps, not from drawn detail. Strictly black structure, neutral grey secondary mass, and white as active negative space. No gradient, no texture, no extra colour. The glyph is very small and exactly centred in a continuous uninterrupted white field, like a museum wayfinding pictogram. Typography, if any, is tiny and at the edge. It is not a large icon, a colourful illustration, a realistic contour drawing, a filled page or a cheap template icon.

These overlay rules add to `references/soldier-runtime.md`. They never replace the source brief or the family runtime contract.

Write every selected final PNG directly inside one fresh task directory under `~/Desktop/xxd/xxd-panel-236/` (or the explicit `--out` root). Use collision-safe filenames; do not create source, mode, or size subdirectories and do not generate an automatic contact sheet.
